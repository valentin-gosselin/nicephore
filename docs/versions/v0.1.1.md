## The local photo viewer, rebuilt

Open a photo or video of your computer with Nicéphore (from your file manager, or as your default viewer):

- **Zoom** with the wheel around the pointer, double click for 100 %, `+` `-` `0` `1` on the keyboard, drag to move around.
- **Full screen** (`F`, `F11`, `Esc` to leave): the bars fade out with the pointer.
- **Right-click menu**: copy the image (`Ctrl+C`) or its path (`Ctrl+Shift+C`), show in folder, open with another app, rotate, slideshow, details, send to Immich or into an album, open in the library, move to the trash.
- **Rotation saved in the file** (`R`, `Shift+R`), without any quality loss for JPEG (only the EXIF orientation changes). PNG, TIFF, BMP and WebP are re-encoded losslessly; RAW and GIF files are never modified.
- **Rotation on Immich too**: a photo already on your server is turned there by the Immich editor, its original kept as sent. It stays the same photo for the sync, never uploaded again as a duplicate.
- **Strip of the folder's photos**, shown or hidden with one button (the choice is remembered).
- **Details panel** (`I`): date taken, place, size, folder, camera, lens and settings.
- **Slideshow** (`D`), **system trash** (`Delete`) with undo, and **drag** the photo to another app.
- The Immich status is now a single icon: a green cloud opens the photo in your library, an orange one sends it.

In the library viewer, a right-click menu too: copy the image, save the original, favorite, add to album, slideshow, full screen, details, trash.

## Fixes

- A viewer window closed right after opening a photo could come back by itself a few seconds later.
- The same photo opened again after closing the window showed a black screen.
- Photos turned or cropped in the Immich web app now show that way in Nicéphore too.

## Good to know

- Rotation on the server uses the Immich editor, available since Immich v2.5 (still marked beta by Immich). On an older server the file is turned and a message says Immich could not be.
- The sync database is upgraded on first launch (a backup copy is kept next to it): going back to 0.1.0 afterwards is not possible.
- The Windows executable is still not code-signed: SmartScreen may warn on first launch ("More info", then "Run anyway").
